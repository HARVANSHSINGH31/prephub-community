# PrepHub Community

**One roadmap. Every round. Written by the people who actually sat in the interview.**

PrepHub Community is an open, campus-rooted repository where employees and alumni write down what they know about getting into their companies — the skills that actually matter, the rounds they went through, and the questions they were genuinely asked — so the next batch of students isn't preparing blind.

This is not a mentorship marketplace. Nobody has to "match" with anyone or book a call. It's a living, version-controlled library: alumni write it once, everyone after them benefits, and it gets corrected and updated the same way open-source code does — by pull request.

## Why this exists

Every campus already has this knowledge. It just lives in scattered WhatsApp groups, one-off senior-junior conversations, and Instagram DMs that get lost the moment the batch graduates. PrepHub Community puts it in one place, attributed to a real, verified person, dated, and kept fresh — so it survives past any one batch.

## What's in this repo

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `companies/` | One guide per company + role: required skills, interview rounds, real question types, and prep resources — written by a verified alum or employee |
| `roadmaps/` | Skill roadmaps per role (SDE, Data Analyst, ML, etc.) — what to learn, in what order, before you're interview-ready |
| `resources/` | Shared prep material: resume checklists, aptitude basics, mock-interview format guides |
| `VERIFIED_CONTRIBUTORS.md` | The registry of everyone who has written a guide — their batch, current company, and how to reach them |
| `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/` | Templates for requesting a new company guide or flagging one that's gone stale |

## How to get help

1. Start with `roadmaps/` for your target role to know what to learn.
2. Read the relevant guide under `companies/<company-name>/`.
3. Have a question the guide doesn't answer? Open a thread in this repo's **Discussions → Ask an Alum** tab, tagging the guide's author. Answers stay public so the next student with the same question doesn't have to ask again.
4. Think something is outdated or wrong? Open an issue using the **Correction / Update** template, or submit a pull request yourself.

## How to contribute (for employees & alumni)

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md). Short version: copy `companies/TEMPLATE.md`, fill it in, get verified once, and submit a pull request. Takes about 20 minutes and helps every future student from your campus.

## What makes this different from GfG, Glassdoor, or a random WhatsApp group

- **Verified authorship.** Every guide is signed by a real, verified alum — not an anonymous handle.
- **Freshness, not staleness.** Every guide carries a `last_updated` and `still_accurate_as_of` field. Old, unconfirmed guides get flagged, not left to rot like an abandoned forum thread.
- **Structured, not a firehose.** Organized by company → role → round, paired with a skill roadmap — not a wall of unsorted Q&A.
- **Doubts stay public.** Questions and answers happen in Discussions, attached to the guide, so the same answer helps every student who reads it after — not just the one who asked.
- **Campus-rooted trust.** This starts with one campus's own alumni writing for their own juniors — not strangers on the internet.
