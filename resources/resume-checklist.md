# Resume & Project Vetting Checklist

## Resume
- [ ] One page, no exceptions, for anyone under 3 years of experience
- [ ] Every bullet starts with an action verb and ends with an outcome or number where possible
- [ ] No skill listed that you can't survive 5 minutes of follow-up questions on
- [ ] Projects section links to live demos or repos, not just names

## Projects
- [ ] You can explain *why* you built it, not just what it does
- [ ] You can explain one hard technical decision you made and what you'd change now
- [ ] At least one project shows depth (not five shallow tutorial clones)

## Before the interview
- [ ] Re-read your own resume the night before — interviewers will ask about anything on it
- [ ] Have a 60-second answer ready for "walk me through this project"
- [ ] Know the numbers you claimed (users, performance improvement, etc.) well enough to justify them
